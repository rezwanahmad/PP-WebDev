### C. Body

#### 1 · First, the two-permit reality
Turkey does not run a one-sticker system. Your **student visa** — issued in Pakistan — only gets you through the airport. The permission that lets you legally *live* in Turkey is the **Student Residence Permit (İkamet İzni)**, applied for online *after* arrival, within 30 days. Every year, students mix these up and land in paperwork trouble in month one. So this checklist has two stages: **Stage A (in Pakistan)** and **Stage B (in Turkey)**. Collect for both before you fly — most Stage B documents are Stage A documents reused.

#### 2 · Stage A — the visa file, document by document

| # | Document | Exact specification | What goes wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Passport | Valid **at least 60 days beyond** your intended stay — realistically keep 18+ months | Short-validity passports stall the whole file |
| 2 | Visa application form | Completed via the online pre-application, printed, signed | Handwritten corrections on printouts get returned |
| 3 | Biometric photos | **2 recent photos, 5×5 cm**, white background | Studios default to passport size — insist on 5×5 |
| 4 | Acceptance letter | Original university letter (programme, duration, fees) | Unconditional letter required — conditional offers don't carry the file |
| 5 | Academic documents | SSC/Matric, HSSC/Intermediate (and degree for PG): **Board-issued, then the attestation chain below** | Photocopies without the chain are decorative, not documents |
| 6 | Funds evidence | Sponsor's bank statements **3–6 months** + signed sponsor affidavit; benchmark ≈ **US$800 per month** of your study period *(indicative)* | Sudden lump-sum deposits raise questions; steady balances read better |
| 7 | Accommodation proof | University dormitory letter, notarised rental contract, or host letter | Vague "hotel booking" for a 4-year degree invites refusal |
| 8 | Health/travel insurance | Covering your **entire stated stay**, valid in Turkey | Policies that end before the course does |
| 9 | Flight itinerary | A **reserved** round-trip itinerary — purchase is not required | Buying a full ticket before a visa decision |
| 10 | Police character certificate | Issued locally, **MOFA-attested** | Skipped because "nobody asked last year" — it's listed now |
| 11 | **Polio vaccination certificate** | Valid WHO-format certificate issued in Pakistan | The single most-forgotten item on Pakistani files |
| 12 | Family Registration Certificate (FRC) | NADRA-issued, current | Ordered late — NADRA processing eats a week |
| 13 | Fee payment receipt | Visa fee + centre charges paid at filing | — |

#### 3 · The attestation chain (denklik trail) — the step agents "forget"
Your certificates mean nothing to a Turkish officer until they climb a five-step ladder:

| Step | Authority | For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your education **Board** (BISE) / university | Verification of SSC/HSSC/degree |
| 2 | **IBCC** | Equivalence stamp for school certificates |
| 3 | **HEC** | Attestation of university degrees/transcripts (PG applicants) |
| 4 | **MOFA Pakistan** | Foreign-office attestation over all of the above |
| 5 | **Turkish mission** (Embassy/consulate or authorised centre) | Consular attestation; universities may additionally run **denklik** (equivalence) |

Budget **2–6 weeks** and PKR 5,000–10,000 *(indicative)* for this chain, and start it the week you apply to universities — not after the acceptance letter. Path Plaza calendars this chain into every Turkey file on day one.

#### 4 · The items nobody mentions (until it's late)
Three of the thirteen items above cause most last-minute panics, simply because they're Pakistan-specific and rarely advertised: the **polio certificate** (mandatory for travellers from Pakistan), the **FRC** (NADRA's family record — needed to show sponsor relationship), and the **5×5 cm photo size** (standard Pakistani passport photos are rejected). All three are cheap and fast — if you know about them early.

#### 5 · Funds evidence — honest expectations
Turkey does not demand an Ireland-style frozen €10,000 window. Guidance-level expectation is around **US$800 per month** of your study period *(indicative benchmark)*, shown through a parent/sponsor's **3–6 months of stable statements**, plus their signed affidavit and CNIC copy. Credibility beats size: a steady salaried account outperforms a fat account filled the week before filing. If your sponsor is self-employed, attach business proof; inconsistency between income story and balance is the quiet refusal trigger.

#### 7 · Stage B — after you land: the İkamet file
Within **30 days of arrival**:
1. **Student certificate** from your university (issued at enrolment).
2. **e-İkamet online application** at the DGMM portal → appointment at the provincial migration office (İl Göç İdaresi).
3. Take: passport + copies, 4 biometric photos, address proof (dorm letter / notarised rent contract), your insurance, and a **Turkish tax number** (free, obtained first — you need it to pay fees).
4. Pay the residence tax + card fee (≈ **US$80–100 equivalent in TRY** — the lira fee re-indexes yearly) through official payment channels; keep the receipt.
5. Your **İkamet card and YKN (foreigner ID number)** follow — that number unlocks bank accounts, SIM contracts and dorm registration. Renew annually against enrolment; your attendance and results are read at renewal.

#### 8 · Seven mistakes that sink Turkish student files
1. Applying inside the last 4 weeks — the calendar, not the officer, refuses you.
2. Standard passport-size photos instead of 5×5 cm.
3. No polio certificate, or an expired one.
4. Certificates attested by the Board but never by MOFA/the Turkish mission.
5. Insurance that ends before the course dates.
6. A sponsor balance inflated by one giant deposit last week.
7. Treating the İkamet as optional — it is the legal basis of your stay, on a 30-day clock.

### G. FAQs (4 — FAQPage schema, text == schema)

1. **How much bank statement do I need for a Turkey student visa from Pakistan?**
   Guidance benchmarks around US$800 per month of your study period, shown through 3–6 months of a sponsor's stable statements with a signed affidavit. Files are judged on credibility — steady balances beat sudden large deposits. *(Indicative; verified at filing.)*

2. **Is the polio certificate really mandatory for Pakistani students?**
   Yes — travellers from Pakistan are required to carry a valid polio vaccination certificate, and it belongs in your visa file. It is cheap and quick in Pakistan; the only cost of skipping it is a stalled application.

3. **Do my documents need Turkish translation?**
   English-language documents are broadly accepted along the attestation chain (Board → IBCC/HEC → MOFA → Turkish mission). Anything issued in Urdu must be professionally translated. Your university can also request equivalence (denklik) — start that chain weeks before your appointment.

4. **What happens if I don't apply for the İkamet within 30 days?**
   You become unlawfully resident — which risks fines, removal, and future entry bans. The 30-day clock is real. We schedule clients' e-İkamet steps as part of the pre-departure brief so the appointment is booked within week one of arrival.
