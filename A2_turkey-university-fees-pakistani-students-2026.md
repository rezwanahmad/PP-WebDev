### C. Body

#### 1 · Rule one: think in dollars, never in lira
Turkish universities publish international fees in **US dollars or euros** for one reason: the lira moves too fast to price a 4-year degree in it. Any fee quoted to you only in TRY is either a local price (not yours) or a number that will be "re-calculated" at your expense later. So this entire article prices in USD with PKR conversion at a working rate of **≈ PKR 280/$** *(18 Aug 2026)*. When you actually pay, the day's rate rules — plan a 5–8% buffer for movement, not optimism.

#### 2 · Tuition: the three tiers (the whole market in one table)

| Tier | Annual tuition (USD) | ≈ PKR/year | Who it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public universities** (TR-YÖS/SAT entry, mostly Turkish-medium) | a few hundred – $3,000 | ≈ 1–8 lakh | Strong academics ready for the exam + Turkish |
| **Private universities — standard** (English-medium tracks common) | $2,500–8,000 | ≈ 7–22 lakh | Most Pakistani applicants |
| **Private — medicine/dentistry** | $9,000–20,000+ | ≈ 25–56 lakh+ | Separate budget conversation entirely |
| **Masters (thesis/non-thesis)** | $3,000–9,000 total-ish | ≈ 8–25 lakh | 1–2 year programmes |

Two honest footnotes. First, within the standard private tier, **merit discounts of 25–75% off the list price are common** — but they only exist when written on your official offer letter; a verbal discount is a story, not a discount. Second, a **prep year** (English or Turkish) adds a full extra year of tuition plus living to whatever number you're calculating — always ask "is my fee per academic year, and does it include the prep year?"

#### 3 · Living costs: city decides more than lifestyle

| City | Monthly total incl. dorm (USD) | ≈ PKR/month |
|---|---|---|
| Istanbul | $350–550 | ≈ 1.0–1.5 lakh |
| Ankara / Izmir | $280–450 | ≈ 0.8–1.3 lakh |

University dorms are the single biggest saver — typically half the cost of a private shared flat, bills included. Istanbul is the most expensive city in this table *and* still roughly half of Dublin; that context is why Turkey-and-Malaysia sit in our "value corridor."

#### 5 · The degree-length arithmetic (where families actually get surprised)
A Turkish bachelor's is **4 academic years** (medicine 6). So the real question isn't "what's the yearly fee?" — it's "what does the *degree* cost?":

| Profile | Year cost (tuition + living, USD) | 4-year all-in ≈ |
|---|---|---|
| Budget: discount private uni, smaller city | $5,000–7,500 | **≈ PKR 56–84 lakh** |
| Standard: private uni, Istanbul/Ankara | $7,000–13,000 | **≈ PKR 78–146 lakh** |
| Medicine (private, Istanbul) | $14,000–26,000 | **≈ PKR 2.0–2.7 crore (6 yrs)** |

For the same honest comparison: a 3–4 year degree in the UK or Ireland typically lands at **PKR 1.0–1.2 crore+** per our current all-in estimates — Turkey's value case is real, but it is a "same-order-of-magnitude, lower" case, not the "almost free" case some Facebook ads sell.

#### 6 · What pulls these numbers down (legitimately)
- **Merit discounts (25–75%):** tied to FSc/A-Level performance at most private universities; result arrives with the offer letter — in writing or it doesn't count.
- **Türkiye Burslari:** the national full scholarship covers tuition + dorm + monthly stipend + flights + a funded Turkish year. It is also selective at single-digit acceptance — treat it as a lottery you *should* enter with a serious file, never as your only plan. (Full guide + honest odds: see our separate Burslari post.)
- **Dorm + smaller city choice:** quietly saves $1,500–2,500 a year versus Istanbul private housing.
- **Instalments:** most private universities split annual fees into 2–4 instalments; the fee plan is part of the offer letter — read it.

#### 8 · Red flags in any fee conversation (memorise these)
1. A total quoted **only in lira** — see rule one.
2. "Everything included" with no itemisation — itemise or walk.
3. Any payment request before an official offer letter exists.
4. A "50% scholarship" that doesn't appear on the offer letter.

### G. FAQs (4 — FAQPage schema)

1. **What is the cheapest realistic university option in Turkey for Pakistani students?**
   A discounted private university in a smaller city: roughly US$5,000–7,500 all-in per year (≈ PKR 14–21 lakh) with dorm living. Public universities cost less in tuition but require the TR-YÖS exam and mostly Turkish-medium study — cheapest in money, not in effort.

2. **Do I pay the full year's fee upfront?**
   Usually no — most private universities split the annual fee into 2–4 instalments and some ask only for a deposit before visa documents. Your payment schedule is written in the offer letter; never pay "cash per semester" to an agent personally.

3. **Can part-time work cover my Turkey fees?**
   No — and be suspicious of anyone who says otherwise. Bachelor's students may work only after year one, capped around 24 hrs/week under current rules, with employer permits and limited Urdu-only options. Budget Turkey as fully family-funded; any later earnings are pocket money, not tuition.

4. **Is Türkiye Burslari worth applying for if it covers everything?**
   Yes — it genuinely covers tuition, dorm, stipend and flights, so apply with a serious file. But acceptance sits in single digits nationally, so it must run alongside a funded Plan B, never instead of one. The 2027-entry window is expected around January–February 2027.
