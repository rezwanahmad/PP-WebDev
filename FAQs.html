01Your service agreement governs

Before any paid work begins, Path Plaza confirms your scope, stages, and fees in a written service agreement (shared and acknowledged in writing, including by email or WhatsApp). That document is the governing record for your case. This page explains the standard framework; if your signed agreement and this page ever differ, your agreement prevails.

03Included vs excluded

Every paid Path Plaza service includes

  • Honest assessment — we would rather lose a sale than sell an unrealistic plan;
  • Official-source verification of fees, requirements, and intakes before you commit money to an application;
  • Organised documentation support and honest file preparation;
  • A written record of scope, stage, and progress for your case;
  • Receipts for every payment made to Path Plaza.

Every Path Plaza service excludes

  • Third-party costs — university application fees, tuition deposits, IELTS/PTE or other test fees, TB test fees, health insurance/IHS-type charges, visa and immigration fees, translations, attestations, notarisation, and couriers. These are set by third parties, paid to them through their official channels, and are usually non-refundable by them.
  • Outcomes — admission, scholarships, visa approval, work, or settlement. Our fees pay for defined work and honest representation of your case, never for a result.
  • Legal or immigration representation, appeals, or tribunal work — where your case needs a qualified lawyer or licensed adviser, we will say so plainly.

04How fees work

  • Fees are discussed in consultation and confirmed in writing before you pay. We do not publish fee schedules on the Website, because scope differs from case to case.
  • Fees are charged per service stage, so you never pay for the whole journey upfront.
  • Fees are never linked to outcomes — no "pay when the visa arrives" promises, and no extra charge if you get a scholarship. Success-dependent pricing is a red flag in this industry; we don't use it.
  • Promotional adjustments (for example, a Roadmap fee credit against a later package) are honoured exactly as written in your agreement.

05Cancellations & refund table Standard framework

SituationRefund position
You cancel the Roadmap before the session / before work begins Full refund of the Roadmap fee.
You cancel after the Roadmap has been delivered No refund — a delivered written plan is completed work. (Any package credit promised in your agreement still stands.)
You withdraw during the application stage Refund of the stage fee minus the value of work already performed (assessments completed, shortlists prepared, applications drafted or submitted), itemised in writing.
You withdraw during the visa-file stage Same principle: unearned portion refunded, completed work retained, itemisation provided.
University or visa refusal A third party's decision does not itself create a refund entitlement — our fee covered the preparation work. Partial refunds apply only where written in your agreement.
Third-party amounts already paid out (application fees, test fees, etc.) Never refundable by us — they belong to the third party's own refund policy, which we will help you check.
Termination for dishonesty (false documents, concealed refusals) Service stops immediately; fees for work performed are not refundable.

06If we cancel a service

Occasionally we must decline or stop a case — for example when requested options are not realistically available, destinations fall outside our four active countries, or a client cannot meet document deadlines. Where Path Plaza ends a service for reasons other than client dishonesty, you receive a refund of all unearned fees for work not yet performed, itemised in writing. Declining an unrealistic case before payment costs you nothing.

07How to request a refund

  • Write to info@pathplaza.com or WhatsApp +92 315 5009620 with your name, the service, and the reason. Written requests create a record for both of us.
  • We acknowledge within 3 working days and give a substantive decision within 14 days, including an itemisation if any portion is retained.
  • Approved refunds are paid by the same channel you paid with, within 14 days of approval, and confirmed in writing.

08Fair-use & honesty clause

Refunds exist for genuine situations. They do not cover: change of mind after completed work; inability to meet third-party costs or entry requirements that were disclosed to you; decisions made by institutions or authorities; or cases terminated for dishonesty. This clause protects honest clients by keeping the service viable and fairly priced for everyone.
