## Page promise

Teach Pakistani students how to separate the process into two files:

1. The student-visa file prepared in Pakistan.
2. The student-residence-permit file prepared after arrival in Türkiye.

The article must be a planning checklist, not a statement that every applicant receives the same document list.

## Mandatory visibility/compliance corrections

- Do not publish “13 documents are always required” as a universal rule. Label the Stage-A list as a practical planning checklist and direct readers to the current consular/mission checklist.
- Do not publish a universal US$800-per-month funds requirement without a current official mission source.
- Do not publish a blanket “apply for İkamet within 30 days” rule. Tell readers to start promptly through the university/international office and apply within their lawful-stay period using current official instructions.
- Distinguish visa-photo requirements from current e-İkamet photo requirements. The official student-residence list currently states two recent ICAO-compliant biometric photographs.
- Treat FRC, police certificate, translations and attestations as case/document-dependent unless the current mission checklist makes them mandatory.
- Keep the Pakistan polio certificate instruction because Pakistan's official polio programme states that outbound international travellers need proof of vaccination.
- Do not publish fixed visa processing or fee figures unless checked on the current mission/authorised-centre page on publication day.
- No approval chances, no guaranteed timeline and no statement that employment can fund study.
