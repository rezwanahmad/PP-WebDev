## 5. Mandatory corrections to the supplied deck

The HTML build must not render these deck claims unchanged:

1. **“Think in dollars, never in lira”** is too broad. Private universities often publish international fees in USD, while public institutions may publish TRY schedules. Rewrite as a currency-verification rule.
2. **Medicine/dentistry US$9,000–20,000+** understates current official examples. Use a current example range of approximately **US$15,000–29,000** across the checked Okan and İstinye pages, clearly framed as examples rather than a national tariff.
3. **“Merit discounts of 25–75% are common”** becomes: some universities publish discount/scholarship tiers, but eligibility is institution-specific and only the official offer/payment schedule controls.
4. Remove all **single-digit acceptance/probability** language for Türkiye Scholarships. State that it is competitive and not guaranteed; do not publish an acceptance rate without an official current dataset.
5. Remove the unsupported **24-hours-per-week** statement. State only what the official law supports: a work permit is required and associate/undergraduate work rights begin after the first year.
6. Do not claim 2027 scholarship dates. Link to the official portal and say the next window must be confirmed when announced.
7. Remove “24–48 hours” from the CTA unless an approved operational service standard is supplied.
8. Replace market-wide certainty with **official fee-page examples + illustrative family budgets**.
9. Label all living, travel and one-time cost figures as planning models from the approved deck, not official national rates.
