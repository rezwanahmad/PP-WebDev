# PP-WEBDEV Standing Delivery Instruction

**Recorded:** 21 August 2026  
**Applies to:** All future Path Plaza blog posts and coding tasks in this workspace.

## Standing rule

For every Path Plaza blog post and every future request to write or modify code, PP-WEBDEV must use the approved developer persona, the Blog Section Developer Brief and the Blog Publishing Guide as governing delivery references.

## Every blog-post delivery must include

- Final self-contained article HTML using the approved article template.
- The same-turn updated `blog.html` with a visible card, correct filters/counts, featured-slot decision, internal links and Blog JSON-LD.
- The same-turn updated `sitemap.xml` with the article canonical, real `lastmod`, and multilingual annotations when applicable.
- A release bundle containing the article, updated `blog.html`, updated `sitemap.xml`, required assets and deployment checklist.
- The article is not considered complete or deployable unless the article file, blog index and sitemap pass together.
- SEO title, meta description, canonical, Open Graph metadata and valid BlogPosting/Breadcrumb schema.
- Real publication and modification dates only.
- Visible category, reading time and content-review date.
- Key-facts box with dated and labelled changeable figures.
- Mandatory “What can change” caveat.
- At least two checked official sources.
- Mid-article WhatsApp CTA and end Initial Profile Check CTA.
- Required reviewed-by/byline block.
- Related posts and previous/next links, or a clear note when unavailable.
- Sitemap entry with real `lastmod`.
- Companion publishing/deployment notes and a completed compliance/QA checklist.
- No internal developer instructions published inside the public-facing article.

## Standing Urdu-content rule

For every Urdu-language Path Plaza page or post:

- Use Urdu script unless management explicitly requests Roman Urdu.
- Write in simple, plain, everyday Urdu with short sentences and a natural free-flowing teaching voice.
- Explain one idea at a time using lesson labels, examples and worked calculations.
- Use an infographic-first layout: summary cards, process flows, timelines, comparison bars, checklists and warning tiles wherever they improve understanding.
- Keep exact figures available in accessible tables as well as visuals.
- Use `lang="ur-PK"`, RTL direction and the approved local Nastaleeq font.
- Isolate English names, currencies, URLs and numbers for correct LTR rendering.
- Never auto-translate mechanically; preserve the same source, date and compliance standards as the English page.

## Every coding delivery must include

- The requested code or updated file.
- A concise change summary.
- Relevant deployment or integration instructions.
- Responsive, accessibility, SEO and performance checks where applicable.
- Pathfinder claim/contact/compliance scan where the code is public-facing.
- Link, schema and structural validation where applicable.
- Any dependencies, unresolved placeholders or missing source material clearly disclosed.
