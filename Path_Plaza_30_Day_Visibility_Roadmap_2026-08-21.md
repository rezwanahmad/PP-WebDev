# Path Plaza — 30-Day Search & AI Visibility Roadmap

**Mode:** PP-WEBDEV v2.0 · PP-VISIBILITY-first  
**Period:** 21 August–19 September 2026 (Asia/Karachi)  
**Prepared:** 21 August 2026  
**Scope:** SEO · AEO · GEO · LLMO · local/entity consistency · content · measurement

## Executive summary

1. The first 72 hours are a safety release: remove the live internal-only contact, repair homepage claims and verify the corrected shell across the site.
2. Public blog output currently exposes empty/template text to crawlers; remove it before requesting broader indexing.
3. The live sitemap contains blog URLs but does not yet represent the full destination/legal inventory.
4. All four country pages are live; the next priority is source, claim, schema and internal-link repair—not more unverified volume.
5. A1 is live. A2 and A3 are conditional on approved copy decks and fresh source ledgers; no ghost URLs or dates.
6. Week 3 turns the destination pages and three pilot posts into one linked answer cluster.
7. Week 4 adds entity consistency, AEO capsules, LLM discovery files, crawler governance and discovery submissions.
8. Day 29 establishes the first reproducible AI-visibility baseline; Day 30 reports evidence, not assumed rankings.
9. This roadmap does not promise page-one rankings or AI citations within 30 days. It builds the conditions and measurement system required to earn them.

- Live pages checked on 21 August 2026: home, destinations, four country pages, blog index, A1 article, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
- Workspace governance checked: PP-WEBDEV v2.0, PP-VISIBILITY v1.0 source persona, blog brief, article template, publishing guide and current sitemap.

## 30-day outcomes sought

- Zero verified internal-only contact exposure on public pages.
- Zero crawler-visible template tokens, empty article inventory or intentional broken links.
- A validated sitemap containing every live canonical URL exactly once.
- Four country pages with current source/claim/schema checks and coherent internal links.
- A1 retained and up to two additional pilot posts published only after approved decks and source verification.
- AEO answer-capsule and FAQ parity across the strongest pages.
- Review-stage or approved live llms.txt/llms-full.txt plus a documented AI-crawler policy.
- GSC/GA4 status, discovery submission log and first AI-visibility probe baseline recorded.

## Roadmap by phase

### Phase 1 — Days 1–7: safety, trust and indexability

Remove the live contact violation, repair the homepage claim stack, remove crawler-visible blog placeholders, rebuild the sitemap and establish measurement access. Do not amplify the site through outreach or indexing requests before these gates are clean.

### Phase 2 — Days 8–14: destination cluster repair

Audit and patch UK, Ireland, Malaysia and Turkey pages using current official sources. Remove unsupported superlatives and behavioural/outcome claims, enforce date labels, and connect each destination page with the hub, relevant blog content and Profile Review.

### Phase 3 — Days 15–21: approved content production

Publish A2 and A3 only if approved Markdown copy decks and source ledgers arrive. Every release includes index card, featured-slot decision, schema, sitemap, internal links, distribution excerpts and post-specific QA guide.

### Phase 4 — Days 22–30: AEO, GEO, LLMO, entity and measurement

Retrofit answer capsules, complete accessibility/performance review, clean NAP/GBP signals, prepare LLM discovery files and crawler policy, submit final URLs and create the first AI-visibility baseline.

### PP-WEBDEV v2.0

- Remove indexable placeholders and rebuild crawl assets.
- Produce exact country-page issue registers and release files.
- Keep source ledgers, schema parity and deployment notes with every change.

### Management / Aneela Usman

- Approve public replacement copy and production windows.
- Provide GSC, GBP and analytics decisions/access through approved channels.
- Approve A2/A3 copy decks and publication dates.
- Approve or reject AI-crawler and llms.txt publication.

### Human deployer

- Deploy only approved files in the stated order.
- Purge caches and verify production URLs.
- Retain rollback copies and release timestamps.

## Non-negotiable release gates

- No `+44`, `7577` or internal-location exposure.
- No guarantee, approval-probability, invented partnership/team or unsupported superlative.
- No public Path Plaza service fee.
- No article without real date, approved deck, source checks and sitemap entry.
- No FAQ schema without matching visible FAQ text.
- No crawler-visible template tokens or noindex/index mismatch.
- No new external tracker without management approval.
- No ranking, traffic or AI-citation claim without data.
