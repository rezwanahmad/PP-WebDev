## §4 — Knowledge domains

### 4.1 Classic SEO
Keyword research & mapping (tier system from the SEO Guide) · search intent classification · on-page (titles/meta/H-structure/internal links/images-alt) · technical (crawlability, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, canonicals, redirects, 404s, pagination, CWV budgets per Brand Kit ≤350KB pages) · local SEO (GBP discipline, NAP consistency, citations — clean directories only, review engine with zero incentivisation) · link acquisition per SEO Guide Part 7 (clean-only: citations, university listings where genuine, guest value-posts, digital PR; **no PBNs/paid link schemes**).

### 4.2 AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation
Featured-snippet & PAA capture: question-led H2/H3, **40–60-word answer capsules** directly under question headings, lists/tables for comparisons, FAQ sections with FAQPage schema **matching visible text exactly**, HowTo schema for process posts, speakable-friendly phrasing, definition boxes ("What is İkamet?"), date-stamping every factual claim.

### 4.3 GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation
Be cite-worthy to generative engines: original proprietary statistics ("our register of 11,205 courses shows…" — always indicative-labelled + dated) · quotable one-sentence answers · named entities used consistently (Path Plaza (SMC) Pvt Ltd / Lahore / four destinations) · third-party citations (official sources linked out: gov.uk, irishimmigration.ie, educationmalaysia.gov.tr, mfa.gov.tr, HEC) · brand mention building on high-trust surfaces (forums, Quora/Reddit — helpful, never astroturfed) · author/reviewer bylines (E-E-A-T) · comparison tables engines can lift · unique frameworks ("two-permit reality", "attestation chain", "honest odds") that engines attach our name to.

### 4.4 LLMO — LLM-Model Optimisation
`llms.txt` + `llms-full.txt` at root (concise sitemap of most-citable pages with one-line summaries — template in §8) · AI-crawler policy in robots.txt — deliberate allow list (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Bingbot) reviewed quarterly · llm-friendly HTML: semantic markup, semantic chunking (self-contained sections that answer fully), stable URLs, no JS-hidden content on key facts · entity-graph strengthening: Organization schema + sameAs (GBP, socials, directories) · IndexNow/Bing submission cadence (Copilot surface) · knowledge-panel hygiene via consistent entity data.
