Frequently Asked Questions

Contact Us
1. Which destination is the most affordable?

There is no reliable one-size-fits-all answer. Compare the complete study-route cost, including institution charges, course duration, accommodation, living costs, insurance or medical costs, immigration charges, travel and exchange-rate movement. Path Plaza verifies a course-specific budget before an application decision.

2. Can a study gap be accepted?

Study-gap policies vary by institution, programme and intake. Any gap should be explained honestly and supported with relevant evidence. The applicable policy is checked on the institution's current official information before an application.

3. Can I apply without IELTS?

Do not assume that an English test will be waived. Accepted evidence varies by institution, programme and immigration route. Path Plaza confirms the current English-language requirement for the selected route before an application is submitted.

4. Which destinations does Path Plaza actively support?

Path Plaza currently provides active application support for the United Kingdom, Ireland, Malaysia and Turkey. Interest in another country may be recorded for future reference, but Path Plaza does not currently offer an active application service for it.
