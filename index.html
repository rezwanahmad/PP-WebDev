1. What makes Path Plaza a good choice for study abroad guidance?

Path Plaza offers ethical, profile-led guidance for the UK, Turkey, Ireland and Malaysia. Our counselors have studied abroad themselves and provide honest, personalized guidance from shortlisting to visa preparation — with no guaranteed outcomes implied.

2. Do you have experienced counselors on your team?

Yes, our counselors have hands-on experience with global university admissions and have personally studied abroad.

3. How long does the university application process take?

Most applications take 4-8 weeks from document preparation to submission. We'll give you a clear timeline during your free consultation.

4. Which countries do you provide guidance for?

Path Plaza currently supports the UK, Turkey, Ireland and Malaysia. You may register interest in another destination, but we are not presently offering an active application service for it.

5. Can you help me get a scholarship?

Yes. We identify scholarships, grants, and financial aid programs that match your profile and help you prepare a strong application.

6. Do you offer visa interview preparation?

Yes. Our team helps you prepare your documentation and practise for the interview. Final visa decisions remain with the relevant immigration authority — we do not guarantee approval.

7. Is the Initial Profile Check really free?

Yes, your Free Initial Profile Check is always free — no pressure, just honest, practical guidance on your options.

8. Do you support students after they arrive abroad?

Yes. We provide pre-departure guidance and our team stays available even after you land.
