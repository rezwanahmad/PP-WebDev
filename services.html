How We Help

Your Journey, Step by Step

A deeper look at how Path Plaza supports you through every critical phase of studying abroad.

Step 1

Free Career & Academic Counselling

We start by understanding you — your academic background, your goals, your budget, and your dream destination. This session shapes everything that follows.

Our experienced counselors have studied abroad themselves, so they give you honest, practical guidance — not just what you want to hear.

  • Detailed academic assessment
  • Career goal mapping
  • Budget and funding discussion
  • Country and course recommendations
Step 2

University Shortlisting & Application

We match your profile with universities that are the right fit — not just the most famous ones. We consider entry requirements, course quality, cost of living, and career outcomes.

Then we help you build a strong application — from SOPs and recommendation letters to transcripts and portfolios.

  • Profile-matched university list
  • Application document preparation
  • SOP and personal statement guidance
  • Deadline management
Step 3

Scholarship, Visa & Pre-Departure

Once you receive an offer, we help you find and apply for scholarships and fee discounts.. Then we guide you through every visa step — documentation, timelines, and interview prep.

Finally, we prepare you for life abroad — accommodation, banking, insurance, and cultural tips — so you land confident and ready.

  • Scholarship identification & application
  • Visa document preparation
  • Interview coaching
  • Pre-departure orientation

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our study abroad services.

What services does Path Plaza provide?
Path Plaza provides complete study abroad support including academic counselling, university selection, application preparation, SOP guidance, scholarship identification, visa support, and pre-departure orientation.
Is the first consultation really free?
Yes, your first consultation is always completely free with no pressure or obligation. It's a chance for us to understand your goals and for you to understand how we can help.
How long does the full application process take?
Timelines vary by country and intake, but most applications take 4-8 weeks from document preparation to submission. We'll give you a clear timeline during your consultation.
Can you help me write my Statement of Purpose (SOP)?
Yes. We guide students in preparing a clear, professional, and compelling SOP or personal statement. We help you structure your story in a way that resonates with university admissions teams.
Do you provide visa interview preparation?
Absolutely. Our visa experts prepare all your documentation and coach you through mock interviews so you're fully prepared and confident on interview day.
Do you help with scholarships?
Yes. We identify scholarships, grants, and financial aid programs that match your profile and help you prepare a strong application to maximize your chances of funding.
