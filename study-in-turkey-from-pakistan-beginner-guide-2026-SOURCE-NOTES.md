## Editorial decisions

- Used the exact current count of 208 universities, with a change warning.
- Replaced the brief’s outdated “some state universities require YÖS” wording with current TR-YÖS/direct-admission guidance.
- Used official master’s and doctorate duration ranges rather than rounded universal figures.
- Kept calendar months approximate and required students to check the exact university calendar.
- Excluded fee figures by design.
- Explained Bologna alignment without treating it as automatic degree recognition.
